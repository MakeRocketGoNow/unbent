# Contributing

Bug reports and ideas are welcome as [issues](https://github.com/MakeRocketGoNow/unbent/issues/new/choose).
Pull requests from outside the project are not being accepted for now, and the repository
only lets collaborators open one. A clear issue (what you did, what you expected, what
happened instead) is the most useful thing you can send.

## When pull requests open again

Unbent is licensed under the [Elastic License 2.0](LICENSE.md). By submitting a
contribution you agree that it is licensed under the same terms, and you grant the licensor
the right to relicense it. That grant is what keeps a future change of terms possible
without tracking down every past contributor.

Opening a pull request is how that agreement is recorded. No sign-off, contributor
license agreement or other paperwork is needed.

For how the code is organized and the conventions it follows, see [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md),
the [README](README.md) and [docs/](docs/README.md).
