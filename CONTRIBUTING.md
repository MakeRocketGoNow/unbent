# Contributing

Unbent is licensed under the [Functional Source License, Version 1.1, Apache 2.0 Future
License](LICENSE.md). By submitting a contribution you agree that it is licensed under the
same terms, and you grant the licensor the right to relicense it. That grant is what lets
each release convert to Apache 2.0 on schedule and keeps a future change of terms possible
without tracking down every past contributor.

Sign your commits off with `git commit -s` to record that agreement (the Developer
Certificate of Origin, <https://developercertificate.org>). No separate contributor
agreement is needed.

For how the code is organized and the conventions it follows, see [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md)
and the [README](README.md).
